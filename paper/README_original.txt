Reproducibility package
=======================

Article title:
  Cross-zone transferability and within-zone recalibration of well-log
  machine-learning predictions of TOC and total gas content, Ordovician
  Wulalike shale, western Ordos Basin

Authors:
  Han Yue, Hexin Huang, Rongxi Li, Siyi Cai

Corresponding authors:
  Rongxi Li    -  rongxli@chd.edu.cn   (principal corresponding author)
  Hexin Huang  -  hx.huang@outlook.com

Affiliation (all authors):
  School of Earth Science and Resources, Chang'an University, Xi'an 710064, China

Package contents
----------------
  supplementary_tables/
      Machine-readable supplementary Tables S1-S30 (CSV format), each cited in
      the main text.

  code/
      Analysis scripts (scripts/), environment specification (env/), fixed random
      seeds, fold definitions, out-of-fold predictions and derived data
      (data_derived/), a file manifest (manifest.csv), and a smoke test
      (test.py) that regenerates the reported metrics and tables from the
      released anonymized derived data.

Data statement
--------------
  The raw well-log, core, and laboratory measurements underlying this study are
  proprietary oilfield data and cannot be redistributed. They are available from
  the corresponding author on reasonable request and with the permission of the
  data owner. Well identifiers in all released files are anonymized.
